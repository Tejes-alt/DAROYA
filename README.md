## `THE CONCEPT`

```text
NOT A LIST OF MISSIONS.
A RECORD OF BECOMING.

ROCKETS
    ↓
ORBIT
    ↓
SATELLITES
    ↓
SCIENCE
    ↓
EXPLORATION
    ↓
HUMAN SPACEFLIGHT
    ↓
2047
```

DAROYA is an interactive space archive built around India's journey
from the foundations of its space programme toward the horizon of 2047.

History is treated as a system rather than a catalogue.

A spacecraft becomes a point in orbit.
A mission becomes a node.
A launcher becomes a lineage.
A scientist becomes part of the network.
A year becomes a coordinate.
